# Destination: Bangalore (Bengaluru)

## Top Attractions
- **Lalbagh Botanical Garden**: 240-acre historic garden with a famous glasshouse inspired by London's Crystal Palace, century-old trees, and lake.
- **Cubbon Park**: The lush green lung of the city, spread across 300 acres in the heart of Bangalore, surrounded by administrative heritage buildings.
- **Bangalore Palace**: Tudor-style royal palace inspired by Windsor Castle, featuring fortified towers, stained glass, and vintage royal memorabilia.
- **Tipu Sultan's Summer Palace**: 18th-century two-storied palace built entirely of teakwood with ornate arches and Indo-Islamic frescoes.
- **Vidhana Soudha**: The seat of the Karnataka state legislature, a majestic neo-Dravidian architectural wonder illuminated on Sunday evenings.
- **Bannerghatta National Park**: Biological park offering jungle safaris (lions, tigers, bears), a zoo, a rescue center, and India's first Butterfly Park.
- **HAL Heritage Centre & Aerospace Museum**: Showcases India's aviation history, aircraft models, fighter jets, and flight simulators.
- **Nandi Hills**: Scenic hill fortress located 60 km from the city, renowned for spectacular sunrise views above the clouds.
- **Church Street & MG Road**: Bustling pedestrian-friendly hub full of bookstores (Blossom Book House), cafes, art galleries, and lively street culture.


## Temples/Churches/Mosques
- **Bull Temple (Dodda Basavana Gudi)**: Basavanagudi - 16th-century Dravidian temple housing a colossal monolithic statue of Nandi the Bull.
- **Dodda Ganapathi Temple**: Adjacent to Bull Temple, famous for its giant monolithic Ganesha statue regularly adorned with butter (Venne Alankara).
- **ISKCON Temple Bangalore**: Rajajinagar - Grand hilltop neo-classical temple complex dedicated to Radha Krishna.
- **St. Mary's Basilica**: Shivajinagar - Bangalore's oldest church, built in Gothic style, renowned for the annual St. Mary's Feast in September.
- **Gavi Gangadhareshwara Temple**: Gavipuram - Cave temple dedicated to Lord Shiva; sunlight illuminates the inner sanctum lingam on Makara Sankranti.
- **Jamia Masjid**: KR Market - The largest and most prominent historic mosque in the city.
- **Infant Jesus Church**: Viveknagar - Celebrated pilgrimage center famous for Thursday novenas.
- **Adiyogi**:Chikkamangluru Kanakapura Road - Tallest statue of Adiyogi in the world, dedicated to Lord Shiva, located on the outskirts of Bangalore.
- **Chikkatirupati**:Kanakapura Road - Temple dedicated to Lord Venkateshwara, known for its ancient architecture and spiritual significance.

## Street Shopping & Markets
- **Commercial Street**: Vibrant paradise for bargain clothing, footwear, traditional jewelry, and accessories.
- **Brigade Road & Church Street**: Trendy fashion boutiques, branded showrooms, vintage bookstores, and handicraft emporiums.
- **Malleshwaram 8th Cross**: Traditional South Indian shopping hub for silk sarees, flowers, brassware, and traditional snacks.
- **KR Market (City Market)**: Historic wholesale flower and spice market with an explosion of colors and fragrances in the early morning.
- **Chickpet & Avenue Road**: Century-old wholesale market for pure Mysore silk sarees, textiles, stationery, and vintage books.

## Local Cuisine
- **Iconic Breakfast Items**: Crisp Benne Dosa (butter dosa), Rava Idli, Medu Vada, Filter Coffee, Bisi Bele Bath, Kesari Bath, Khara Bath.
- **Traditional Karnataka Meals**: Jolada Roti meals, Mangalore Neer Dosa, Kori Rotti, Akki Roti, Mysore Pak, Obbattu / Holige.
- **Legendary Food Institutions**: Vidyarthi Bhavan (Gandhi Bazaar), CTR / Shri Sagar (Malleshwaram), Brahmin's Coffee Bar (Basavanagudi), Mavalli Tiffin Room / MTR (Lalbagh Road), Veena Stores.
- **Pub & Craft Brewery Culture**: Known as the Pub Capital of India; microbreweries in Indiranagar, Koramangala, and Lavelle Road (Toit, Arbor Brewing Company, Windmills Craftworks).

## Culture & Traditions
- **Festivals & Heritage**:
  - **Bangalore Karaga**: Oldest cultural festival celebrated by the Thigala community, honoring Draupadi with a vibrant night procession.
  - **Kadalekai Parishe (Groundnut Fair)**: Annual two-day groundnut fair held in Basavanagudi around the Bull Temple.
  - **Dussehra & Bengaluru Habba**: Celebrations featuring music, classical dance, Yakshagana, and heritage exhibitions.
- **Lifestyle**: Harmonious fusion of classical Kannada traditions with a modern, global IT and startup cafe culture.

## Best Time to Visit & Monsoon Experience
- **October to March**: Peak tourist season with crisp, cool, and pleasant weather (15°C - 28°C).
- **April to May**: Mild summer with occasional pleasant evening thundershowers.
- **June to September (Monsoon)**: Moderate showers transform the city's parks into emerald oases. Mild breezes make it great for cafe visits and garden walks, but expect peak hour traffic delays. Carry an umbrella or light raincoat.

## Budget & Transport
- **Modes of Transport**:
  - **Namma Metro (Purple & Green Lines)**: Air-conditioned, punctual, and the most reliable way to beat city traffic.
  - **BMTC Buses**: Extensive city bus network, including green/blue electric buses and Volvo AC buses. **Vayu Vajra** luxury AC buses connect the airport to all major city hubs.
  - **Auto-Rickshaws & App Cabs**: Auto-rickshaws operate on digital meters (plus night fare after 10 PM) or via Uber/Ola/Namma Yatri apps.
- **Estimated Daily Budget**:
  - **Budget (Backpacker)**: ₹1,000 – ₹2,200/day (Hostels in Indiranagar/Koramangala, BMTC/Metro transport, traditional darshini tiffin centers).
  - **Mid-Range**: ₹3,500 – ₹7,000/day (3-star hotels/B&Bs, Metro/autos/Uber, dining at breweries and heritage cafes).
  - **Luxury**: ₹11,000+/day (5-star luxury hotels like The Leela Palace, ITC Gardenia, chauffeur-driven AC cabs, fine dining).

## Popular Itineraries

### 1-Day Heritage & Garden City Tour
- **Morning (8:00 AM - 12:00 PM)**: Breakfast at MTR or Brahmin's Coffee Bar. Morning stroll through **Lalbagh Botanical Garden** and visit **Bull Temple** in Basavanagudi.
- **Afternoon (12:30 PM - 4:00 PM)**: Explore **Tipu Sultan's Summer Palace** and the grand **Bangalore Palace**. Drive past **Vidhana Soudha** for photography.
- **Evening (4:30 PM - 8:30 PM)**: Relax in **Cubbon Park**, browse bookshops on **Church Street**, and enjoy dinner/craft beer in **MG Road / Lavelle Road**.

### 2-Day Tech, Culture & Cafe Hopping Itinerary
- **Day 1 (Gardens, Palaces & Central Bengaluru)**: Lalbagh, Cubbon Park, National Gallery of Modern Art (NGMA), shopping at Commercial Street, and evening dinner at Church Street.
- **Day 2 (Spirituality, Science & Nightlife)**: Morning visit to **ISKCON Temple**, afternoon tour of **HAL Aerospace Museum**, and evening cafe-hopping and brewery tour in **Indiranagar / Koramangala**.

### 3-Day Nature & Day Excursions Itinerary
- **Day 1**: Central Bangalore Heritage, Cubbon Park, Bangalore Palace & Church Street.
- **Day 2**: Early morning sunrise drive to **Nandi Hills** (visit Bhoga Nandeeshwara Temple at the base), return by afternoon, evening tiffin in Malleshwaram (CTR Benne Dosa).
- **Day 3**: Day trip to **Bannerghatta National Park** for Grand Safari and Butterfly Park. Evening shopping for Mysore Silk at Chickpet or Malleshwaram.

## Travel Tips
- **Navigating Peak Traffic**: Bangalore traffic can be heavy between 8:30 AM–11:30 AM and 5:30 PM–9:00 PM. Rely on **Namma Metro** for longer cross-city commutes.
- **Nandi Hills Travel**: Book entry tickets/parking online via the official portal for weekend mornings to avoid long queues at the checkpoint.
- **Darshini Etiquette**: Traditional breakfast joints (Darshinis) operate on a self-service token system; collect your token at the cash counter before heading to the food counter.
- **Weather Preparedness**: Always keep a light jacket or umbrella handy as sudden, pleasant drizzles are common in the evenings.
